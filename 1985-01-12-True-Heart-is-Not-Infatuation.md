That’s why I said wholeheartedness is exactly the subject, you yourself. Not adverb, not adjective. Not verb; not a word. *You* must be, exactly. 

That practice is *very* quiet practice. Touching to the core of existence, just like a sound of a stream, which you can hear regardless of whether you close your eyes or not, regardless of you cover your ears or not, sound of stream or river is constantly going. You have to stand up *there*. Just practice there. 

I will be here middle of April. I pray for your practice, for your life.
